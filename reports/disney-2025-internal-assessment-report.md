# Disney 2025 Internal Assessment Timeline: Consolidated Findings

## Executive Summary

All 2025 internal notes on Disney (DIS) maintained an **Outperform** rating, with no upgrades or downgrades during the year [1]. The firm’s price target increased steadily from **$115 in February 2025** to **$135 in November 2025** [1]. The revisions were driven primarily by rising earnings estimates and improving fundamentals, rather than multiple expansion, as the target multiple remained around **19x** [1]. The core investment thesis centered on streaming/DTC profitability, durable Experiences performance, and a studio/content rebound, while recurring risks included macro softness, linear decline, sports-rights costs, and pre-opening costs [1].

## Original Research Question / Hypothesis

**Research question:** Did Disney’s internal rating and investment thesis change during 2025, and what drove its quarterly price-target revisions? [1]

**Hypothesis, derived from the provided findings:** Disney’s internal rating would remain stable, while price targets would increase because forward earnings estimates improved rather than because valuation assumptions became more aggressive [1].

## Key Findings

### 1. Rating remained unchanged at Outperform throughout 2025

Disney’s internal assessment maintained an **Outperform** rating across the 2025 review timeline. There were no rating changes, upgrades, or downgrades reported in the consolidated notes [1].

### 2. Price target progression

| **Nov 13, 2025** — Q4 FY25 | **Outperform** | **$135** | Price target raised from $125 as DTC profitability, Experiences growth, and capital returns strengthened. Valuation was rolled forward from FY26E to FY27E, with an approximate multiple of **19x** on **≈ $7.10** of estimated EPS. | [1] |

### 3. The thesis evolved through estimate revisions, not valuation re-rating

The internal notes indicate that each quarter’s price-target increase reflected stronger earnings expectations rather than a more aggressive valuation assumption. The target multiple stayed broadly around **19x**, while the earnings base used for valuation improved through the year [1].

### 4. Core bull thesis was consistent

The underlying investment case remained consistent across 2025 and was anchored around three themes:

- **Streaming/DTC profitability inflection** — improving monetization and profit scaling from direct-to-consumer streaming [1].
- **Experiences durability** — parks and related businesses were viewed as resilient, despite cost and weather-related offsets [1].
- **Studio/content rebound** — content and studio performance supported the broader earnings improvement narrative [1].

### 5. Recurring risk factors flagged

The notes repeatedly identified offsetting risks and concerns, including:

- Macro softness [1].
- Linear television decline [1].
- ESPN and broader sports-rights costs [1].
- Weather-related and cruise pre-opening cost pressures [1].

### 6. Q4 FY25 represented the strongest expression of the thesis

The final 2025 internal update raised the price target to **$135**, citing stronger DTC profitability, continued Experiences growth, and improved capital returns. The valuation framework moved forward to FY27E EPS of approximately **$7.10**, still underpinned by an approximate **19x** multiple [1].

## Bottom Line

The 2025 internal assessment timeline for Disney shows a **stable Outperform rating** and a **progressively strengthening earnings case**. The price target rose from $115 to $135 because each quarterly review raised forward earnings estimates, while the assumed valuation multiple remained broadly unchanged at approximately 19x. The core thesis centered on DTC profitability, durable Experiences, and studio/content recovery, with recurring offsets from macro softness, linear decline, sports-rights costs, and operational cost pressures [1].
